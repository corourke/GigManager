GigWrangler can push your gigs one-way into Google Calendar — your personal
calendar or a shared organization calendar.

## Connect

Open **Settings → Calendar** and connect your Google account.

## Choose a calendar you can write to

The calendar picker only lists calendars you **own** or have **"Make changes to
events"** access on. To sync into a shared or team calendar you don't own, either:

- ask its owner to grant you that access, then reconnect, or
- connect using the owner's Google account.

If a selected calendar later loses write access, GigWrangler shows a warning here
and blocks **Sync All Gigs** until it's resolved.

## What syncs

Gigs sync one-way, GigWrangler → Google. Changes you make in Google Calendar don't
come back.
