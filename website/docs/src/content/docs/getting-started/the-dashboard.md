When you select an organization, you land on its **Dashboard** (Admins and
Managers) with an at-a-glance summary:

- **Gigs** — counts by status (Booked, Proposed, Date Hold).
- **Equipment** — total, insured, and rental value.
- **Revenue** — this month, last month, this year.
- **Status Summary** — Completed, Settled, Cancelled.
- **Upcoming Gigs (Next 30 Days)** — with venue and act.
- **Recent Activity**.

## Navigation

The top bar has: **Dashboard · Gigs · Financials · Team · Equipment**. What you see
depends on your role — Staff and Viewers get a reduced set (no Dashboard or
Financials).

The avatar menu (top-right) has **Switch Organization**, **Settings**,
**Edit Profile**, and **Sign Out**. The bell next to it shows notifications, such
as decisions on [access requests](/getting-started/organizations/).
