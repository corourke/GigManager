Plans change in the warehouse: a spare wedge, an extra box of cables, a whole lighting
kit. You can add equipment to a gig while you pack, from the
[Scanning](/mobile/field-inventory/) screen, and it's tracked like everything else.

Admins, Managers and Staff can add equipment in the **Pack-Out** and **Load Truck**
steps.

## Adding by scanning

Scan the tag of a kit, unit or lot that isn't on the gig.

- **A kit:** "… isn't on this gig." Tap **Add to this gig**. The kit is assigned to
  the gig, with everything in it.
- **A unit:** "… isn't on the list." Tap **Add to this gig**.
- **A lot:** "Not on this gig. How many are going?" Enter the number and tap
  **Add to this gig**.

## Adding by name

1. Tap **+ Add**.
2. Enter a kit, item or lot name. Results are grouped under **Kits** and **Items**.
3. Tap the one you want. For a lot, enter how many.

Something already on the gig is marked "on this gig", and tapping it scans it instead
of adding it twice.

## Checking for conflicts

Before an addition, GigWrangler checks the gig's dates against other gigs. If the
addition is also booked for an overlapping gig, or would leave another gig short,
**Before you pack** says so, such as "Speaker Stand, Tripod: 2 short while Harvest
Gala Dinner & Dance runs." Tap **Pack anyway** to add it, or **Cancel**.

Gear in Maintenance or Inactive is flagged the same way.

## What you added

Additions are listed under **Added at pack-out** on the Scanning screen.

- **Kits** show "· kit". The person who added a kit can **Remove** it while still
  packing, in **Pack-Out** or **Load Truck**.
- **Units and lots** have a circle like any other line. Tap it in each later step,
  such as **Load-In** and **Unload**.

On the gig's page, an added kit has an **Added at pack-out** label on the
**Equipment** card and the packing list. Units and lots added on their own are in a
group of the same name at the end of the packing list.

Kits added at pack-out stay on the gig. To change the kit itself, such as swapping
one unit for another for good, edit the kit afterwards.

## Without a connection

You can still add units and lots. A kit added offline shows "Contents load when
online", and its contents appear once you're back online. The conflict check needs a
connection, so it's skipped offline.
