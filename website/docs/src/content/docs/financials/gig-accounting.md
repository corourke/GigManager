**Financials → Gig Accounting** puts every gig's money in one list. It's for Admins only.

## Sections

| Section | Shows | On by default |
|---|---|---|
| **Needs attention** | Gigs with money due now, and finished gigs with no money in recorded | Yes |
| **Upcoming** | Gigs still to come | Yes |
| **Settled** | Gigs where everything is paid | No |

Turn sections on and off with the buttons that show each section's count. Cancelled gigs with no money recorded are left out.

## Filters

- **Search gigs…** by name.
- **Gig date:** **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **This year** (the default), **Last year**, **All time**, or **Custom** (**From** / **To**).
- **Table view** or **Card view**.

## The figures

The bar at the top totals the gigs on screen:
- **Booked**, **Received** and **Owed to you** for money in. **Owed to you** counts only gigs marked Completed, so money for a gig that hasn't happened yet isn't in it;
- **Costs** and **You owe** for money out;
- **Net**.

Each gig shows:
- **Money in**: received and owed.
- **Costs**: paid, owed, and staff booked.
- **Net**, with its margin.
- A **Status**, such as "No money in recorded" or "You owe $X now".

## Going to a gig

- **Click a gig** to open it on its **Financials** tab.
- **The arrow** expands the gig in place: each money in and money out row with its stage and date, the totals still owed each way, and **View Gig Financials**.
