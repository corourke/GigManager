## Roles

Each membership has one role, and roles are **per organization** — you can be an
Admin of one and a Viewer of another.

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Everything, including managing members and roles, and editing or deleting the organization. |
| **Manager** | Create and run gigs, staff them, manage equipment and financials, invite members. Cannot change Admin roles. |
| **Staff** | See assigned gigs, schedule, and call details; use field inventory. No create/edit/delete of org data. |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to the organization's gigs and team. |

Create/edit/delete controls are hidden for Staff and Viewers. Reading financials
and creating gigs are limited to Admins and Managers.

## Claimed vs. unclaimed organizations

An organization is **claimed** once it has an Admin. Until then it's **unclaimed**
— for example, a venue that was added as a gig participant but that nobody at that
venue has taken over yet.

- **Claimed** — only its Admins and Managers can edit it; only Admins can delete it.
- **Unclaimed** — a [platform moderator](#platform-moderators) handles access
  requests for it, and decides who becomes its first Admin.

See [Getting into an organization](/getting-started/organizations/) for how to
claim one or request access.

## Platform moderators

A small number of accounts have a **platform moderator** flag. They review access
requests for **unclaimed** organizations and approve or reject them from a queue.
They don't otherwise act inside your organization.
