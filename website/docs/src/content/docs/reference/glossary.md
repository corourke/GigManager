The words this guide uses for the things in GigWrangler. Statuses and other values appear as the app shows them.

**Act**
: A performer or group on a gig. Add an organization with the **Act** role and it shows as the gig's act. See [Participating organizations](/gigs/participating-organizations/).

**Admin**
: The role with full control of an organization, including its team, its details and the financial reports only Admins see. See [Roles & access](/reference/roles-and-access/).

**Assignment**
: A person placed in a staff slot on a gig. Each assignment has a status: **Requested**, **Confirmed** or **Declined**. See [Staffing](/gigs/staffing-and-participants/).

**Asset**
: One piece of equipment you own, such as a mixing console or a speaker, tracked individually. See [Equipment & inventory](/equipment/overview/).

**Booked**
: A gig status: the gig is confirmed. See [Status](/gigs/overview/#status).

**Cancelled**
: A gig status: the gig is not happening. See [Status](/gigs/overview/#status).

**Claimed**
: An organization that has an Admin. See **Unclaimed**.

**Client**
: The participating organization that is paying for a gig. Select the star on a participant to **Mark as client**. See [Participating organizations](/gigs/participating-organizations/).

**Completed**
: A gig status: the gig has taken place. See [Status](/gigs/overview/#status).

**Confirmed**
: An assignment status: the person has accepted the slot. Staff confirm or decline their own assignments from their phones. See [Staffing](/gigs/staffing-and-participants/).

**Contact**
: A person you can call at a participating organization for a particular gig, such as a venue's production manager. A contact doesn't need a GigWrangler account. See [Participating organizations](/gigs/participating-organizations/).

**Container**
: A kit whose **Tracking Type** is **Container**: the whole kit, and everything nested in it, is scanned as one unit. The other choice is **Items**, where each component is scanned individually.

**Date Hold**
: A gig status: the dates are held but nothing is committed. New gigs start here. See [Status](/gigs/overview/#status).

**Declined**
: An assignment status: the person turned the slot down. On a gig's money, a booking or cost that fell through. See [Staffing](/gigs/staffing-and-participants/).

**Gig**
: A single job, with its dates, status, participants, schedule, staffing, equipment and money. See [Gigs overview](/gigs/overview/).

**Kit**
: A named group of assets and other kits, such as "FOH rack", that you assign to a gig as one package. Kits can nest inside other kits.

**Manager**
: The role that runs gigs, equipment and financials day to day but can't make or change Admins. See [Roles & access](/reference/roles-and-access/).

**Manifest**
: A printable checklist of gear and where it stands, which you can check off on screen. **Print manifest** on the Locations tab opens one for a location.

**Member**
: A person on an organization's **Team**, with a role. See [Team](/team/overview/).

**Money in / money out**
: A gig's income (bookings and payments) and its costs (expenses, sub-contractors and staff pay). Each item sits at a stage, from quoted to paid. See [A gig's money](/financials/gig-expenses/).

**Organization**
: A company, venue or act that owns its own gigs, team, equipment and financials. A person can belong to several. See [Getting into an organization](/getting-started/organizations/).

**Packing list**
: Every asset to bring for a gig, listed from the kits assigned to it. Open it from the gig's **Equipment** tab.

**Participant**
: An organization taking part in a gig, such as a venue, an act or a vendor, with a role on that gig. Your own organization is added automatically. See [Participating organizations](/gigs/participating-organizations/).

**Platform moderator**
: An account that reviews access requests for unclaimed organizations and maintains the starter category lists. See [Access requests & moderation](/reference/access-requests-and-moderation/).

**Proposed**
: A gig status: you've offered the job but it isn't confirmed. See [Status](/gigs/overview/#status).

**Purchase**
: An invoice or receipt you've recorded, with one line for each item. See [Purchases](/financials/purchases/).

**Requested**
: An assignment status: the person has been asked and hasn't answered yet. See [Staffing](/gigs/staffing-and-participants/).

**Role**
: What you can do in an organization: **Admin**, **Manager**, **Staff** or **Viewer**. Roles are per organization. Don't confuse it with a gig's staff role. See [Roles & access](/reference/roles-and-access/).

**Settled**
: A gig status: the gig is over and its money is squared away. See [Status](/gigs/overview/#status).

**Staff**
: The role for crew: they see their organization's gigs and their own assignments but not pay or financials. See [Roles & access](/reference/roles-and-access/).

**Staff role**
: The job someone does on a gig, such as FOH or Lighting, shown as **Position** on the Team screen. Don't confuse it with the **Staff** role. See [Staffing](/gigs/staffing-and-participants/).

**Staff slot**
: A position to fill on a gig: a staff role and how many people are needed. See [Staffing](/gigs/staffing-and-participants/).

**Unclaimed**
: An organization with no Admin yet, such as a venue added to a gig by someone else. A platform moderator decides who becomes its first Admin. See [Access requests & moderation](/reference/access-requests-and-moderation/).

**Viewer**
: The read-only role. See [Roles & access](/reference/roles-and-access/).
